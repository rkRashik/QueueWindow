# QueueWindow design system

The desk prioritizes the active ticket and next action, with the waiting list beside it on wide screens. At narrow widths the active ticket comes first. Admin uses a quiet section rail, consistently labelled forms, and compact data tables. Auth shares the same controls and pairs them with a slate brand panel.

Plus Jakarta Sans carries English UI and Hind Siliguri carries Bangla. Desk and admin share a persisted language switch. Display tokens, elapsed timers, and metrics use tabular figures. Keep English and Bangla copy comparable in specificity.

Use white panels with a hairline slate border on the off-white canvas. Indigo marks primary actions. Emerald, amber, and rose accompany text labels for called, paused, and destructive states. Keep support copy and secondary controls visually quiet.

The waiting list uses short transitions to clarify entries and exits. Reduced motion removes that movement. Maintain visible focus, native dialog behavior, and independent horizontal scrolling for dense tables.
